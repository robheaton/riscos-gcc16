Upstream reports for the GCCSDK maintainers - bundle of 2026-10-04
==================================================================

Twenty-four reports, one problem per report, in five parts: 01 - 08
(SharedUnixLibrary, UnixLib, ARMEABISupport, the kernel: the serious problems),
09 - 18 (ten smaller UnixLib patches), 19 - 20 (two UnixLib fixes for programs
that run with the DDEUtils module loaded), 21 (one UnixLib fix, found
through the native LTO work; it stands alone) and 22 - 24 (three UnixLib
fixes found while making gcov, profile-guided optimisation and the
semaphore calls work: the .fini_array, getrlimit (RLIMIT_STACK), POSIX
semaphores). Each is a complete
plain-text e-mail (the first lines - From / To / Subject / Attach - are the
mail headers, the body starts after the blank line), with patches,
reproducers and the checks behind them. NOTHING HAS BEEN SENT. Everything is
written in the first person, signed "Rob Heaton"; read them as yours before
sending.

(https://www.riscos.info/index.php/Bug_Reporting) says to post bug reports to
the GCCSDK mailing list.  Its link,
http://www.riscos.info/cgi-bin/mailman/listinfo/gcc, gave "404 Not Found" on

GCCSDK packages (SharedLibs-C-armeabihf 10.2.0-1, from the autobuilder) names
"GCCSDK mailing list <gcc@gccsdk.riscos.info>" as its maintainer, and the
Mailman page above is the list called "gcc": that is very probably the posting
address, and it is what the "To:" lines say now, but I could not check that it
takes mail from non-members.  Please confirm it (subscribe first if the list
requires it).

The sources are the GCCSDK svn trunk (svn://svn.riscos.info/gccsdk/trunk); its
last revision when I looked was r7800 (2024-10-17, Lee Noar), and every patch
applies to that revision with  patch -p1  from the top of the tree (paths
a/gcc4/...).  Report 07 is partly a RISC OS kernel question; its "To:" line
says so.

The reports
-----------
 01  SharedUnixLibrary 1.16: a vfork child that ends without exec kills its
     parent, resizes its parent's Wimp slot and - when the parent was started
     by exec - frees the parent's SOManager client (hard freeze).
     Patch: sul-all-vfork-child (or sul-1, sul-2, sul-3).
 02  UnixLib: the _exit of such a child frees the RMA block of the shared
     program image.  Patch: unixlib-vfork-child-pthread-fini.
 03  UnixLib: the signal stack (one page per program) is never freed.
     Patch: unixlib-free-signal-stack.
 04  ARMEABISupport: a failed mmap leaves its "mmap#N" dynamic area (and the
     pages it claimed) until the next reboot.
     Patch: armeabisupport-mmap-failure-cleanup (against 1.05) and, optional,
     unixlib-mmap-refuse-impossible.
 05  ARMEABISupport + SharedUnixLibrary: the mmap memory of a vfork + exec
     child stays allocated until the ROOT process ends.  No patch (a design
     suggestion).
 06  ARMEABISupport: *RMKill succeeds while SOManager holds the handle of a
     global allocator; every EABI program then fails to start (minor).  No
     patch.
 07  RISC OS 5.30 / ARMEABISupport: a store made by the OS in SVC mode to a
     not-yet-mapped page of an EABI stack is lost; a 64-byte VSTM into such a
     page is not recovered.  No patch (cause not found; the report asks the
     questions).
 08  UnixLib + SharedUnixLibrary: the heap of a vfork + exec child (in the Wimp
     slot) grows over the copy of its parent that SUL keeps at the top of the
     slot: the parent resumes destroyed.  Patch: unixlib-vfork-exec-heap-limit
     (UnixLib only, 76 lines).
 09 UnixLib: fread () / fwrite () do not advance the data pointer after a short
     read () / write (): data stored over itself, or written twice. Latent
     (code reading + a host model of the real functions). Patch:
     unixlib-stdio-short-transfers.
 10 UnixLib: pthread_once () holds one global mutex across the init routine:
     std::async deadlocks; an exception from the routine terminates the
     program. Patch: unixlib-pthread-once (needs -fexceptions for once.c).
 11 UnixLib: pthread_cond_timedwait () deadline from time () (whole seconds);
     wrong base for CLOCK_MONOTONIC: timed waits overrun by up to a second.
     Patch: unixlib-pthread-cond-timedwait.
 12 UnixLib: sleep () / usleep () / nanosleep () with several threads, outside
     the Wimp: one shared alarm and flag. From code reading only. Patch:
     unixlib-sleep-threads (26 lines).
 13 UnixLib: sysconf (_SC_NPROCESSORS_ONLN) returns 2048: the enum numbering
     restarts after _SC_PAGE_SIZE. Patch: unixlib-sysconf-nprocessors.
 14 UnixLib: five implicit function declarations that GCC 14 and later reject.
     Patch: unixlib-gcc14-implicit-declarations.
 15 UnixLib: touch stack buffers before read / fread / recv / recvfrom (a
     workaround for report 07, first problem). Patch:
     unixlib-touch-stack-buffers.
 16 UnixLib: split the 64-byte VSTM of the NEON memcpy / memmove (a workaround
     for report 07, second problem). Patch: unixlib-memcpy-split-vstm.
 17 UnixLib: a main stack bigger than 1 MB for EABI programs (__stack_size),
     with a fallback. Patch: unixlib-eabi-stack-size.
 18 UnixLib: a heap dynamic area that does not fit into the address space is
     retried at half the maximum size. Patch: unixlib-da-heap-fallback.
 19 UnixLib: the inline SWI wrappers of incl-local/internal/os.h read their
     results from local register variables after the asm: with GCC 16 the
     arguments of every program are cut to the length of its name while the
     DDEUtils module is loaded. Patch: unixlib-inline-swi-register-variables
     (738 lines, generated by repro/regvar/fix-unixlib-regvars.py).
 20 UnixLib: __get_dde_prefix () never returns when a DDEUtils prefix is set.
     Patch: unixlib-ddeutils-prefix-loop (one word).
 21 UnixLib: scanf () has no long long conversions: %llx / %lld are cut to 32
     bits (the high word is not written), %hhd writes a short, %jd %zu %td %qd
     are not understood, %Lf stores a float.  This is why the native GCC's
     lto1 could not find the sections of its own objects (sscanf ".%llx").
     Patch: unixlib-scanf-long-long (204 lines, one file).
 22 UnixLib: the .fini_array of an EABI program is never run (the FIXME in
     __main): C destructors do not run, and a program built with --coverage or
     -fprofile-generate never writes its .gcda file (libgcov's exit function
     is a static destructor that GCC adds to every instrumented object).
     Patch: unixlib-fini-array (77 lines, three files).
 23 UnixLib: getrlimit (RLIMIT_STACK) of an EABI program answers the largest
     possible Wimp slot (512 MB), not the size of its main stack (1 MB unless
     __stack_size says more): a recursion that is sized by the limit overflows
     the real stack.  Patch: unixlib-getrlimit-stack (64 lines, three files).
 24 UnixLib: POSIX semaphores: sem_timedwait () is ENOSYS and not declared,
     sem_wait () polls with pthread_yield () and leaks a queue entry per call,
     sem_destroy () never says EBUSY.  Patch: unixlib-semaphores (378 lines,
     two files).
