Subject: Eight problems in SharedUnixLibrary / UnixLib / ARMEABISupport found
         while forward-porting GCCSDK to GCC 16 (reports follow, one per mail)
Attach:  none

Hello,

I have been forward-porting the GCCSDK EABI tool chain (arm-riscos-gnueabihf)
to GCC 16.2.0 and binutils 2.45.1, on top of the UnixLib, SharedUnixLibrary
and ARMEABISupport sources of trunk r7800, and testing it on a Raspberry Pi
Compute Module 4 running RISC OS 5.30 - including native gcc, g++ and
gfortran driven by GNU make on the machine itself.  That exercises vfork,
exec and mmap far harder than most programs do, and it turned up eight
problems in SharedUnixLibrary 1.16, UnixLib, ARMEABISupport and, possibly,
the kernel.

I will send them as separate mails, the most serious first:

  1  SharedUnixLibrary: a vfork child that ends without exec kills its
     parent, resizes its parent's Wimp slot and, when the parent was started
     by exec, freezes the machine.  Patch.
  2  UnixLib: the _exit of such a child frees the RMA block of the shared
     program image.  Patch.
  3  UnixLib: the signal stack of every program is never freed.  Patch.
  4  ARMEABISupport: a failed mmap leaves its dynamic area behind until the
     next reboot.  Patch (against 1.05).
  5  ARMEABISupport / SharedUnixLibrary: the mmap memory of a vfork + exec
     child stays allocated until the root process ends.  Design suggestion.
  6  ARMEABISupport: *RMKill while SOManager is loaded (minor).
  7  RISC OS 5.30 / ARMEABISupport: a store made by the OS to a not-yet-mapped
     page of an EABI stack is lost.  Reproducer; I did not find the cause.
  8  UnixLib / SharedUnixLibrary: the heap of a vfork + exec child can grow
     over the saved copy of its parent.  Reproducer.

Each mail is self-contained: what happens, a reproducer, what I measured on
the machine, the cause as far as I could find it in the sources, and a patch
against trunk r7800 where I have one.  I can run a test build of any of the
modules on the machine if that would help.

The analysis, the patches and the drafting were done with the help of an AI
assistant (Claude); every run on the machine was mine.
